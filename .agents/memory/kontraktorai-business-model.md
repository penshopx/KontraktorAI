The user approved these Part 02 recommendations:

- Use a hybrid model combining services and digital products.
- UMKM is Gustafta's broad primary customer market.
- KontraktorAI's initial industrial audience is contractor UMKM and industry
  professionals.
- For the first 12 months, prioritize cash flow from project work and consulting
  while developing SaaS gradually.

The user also confirmed the Part 03 strategy:

- Use an endorsed-brand structure: Gustafta is the master brand and KontraktorAI
  is its endorsed industry brand.
- Follow an UMKM-first, industry-specialized market strategy. Keep the five
  industrial sectors and their phased market entry documented in the brand and
  focus memory.
- Validate customer problems and paid demand before standardizing and
  productizing solutions.

The user confirmed these Part 04 choices:

- Balance services and product development, keeping services as the near-term
  cash-flow path while building digital products and SaaS gradually.
- Choose between no-code, low-code, and custom development according to the
  need rather than following a single fixed implementation method.
- Combine Gustafta-owned products with third-party technology integrations.

Prices, margins, revenue projections, and product readiness remain subject to
market and cost validation; do not present them as final or guaranteed.

The user also finalized Part 05 as a founder-led organization:

- Gustafta is currently operated by one founder.
- The initial workforce model is founder + AI + external specialists.
- Keep operations lean and digital-first; add hiring or fixed costs only when
  recurring workload, capacity constraints, and cashflow support it.
- Human oversight remains necessary for consequential financial, contractual,
  security, and technical-quality decisions.
- Treat the operating roadmap as conditional on workload, pipeline, and cashflow,
  not as a promise to hire or achieve milestones on fixed dates.

For Part 06, the user selected bootstrap / very limited starting capital, without
providing a nominal amount. Keep the financial plan cashflow-first and do not
invent capital, revenue, price, cost, margin, break-even, runway, or forecast
figures. Use formulas and labeled assumptions until actual data is available.

The user also finalized a **service-first** revenue priority for the first 12
months while retaining the hybrid service/product model. The service strategy
is a combination: AI & Digital Consulting opens the customer relationship;
Digital Foundation and Business Automation are the cashflow priorities; AI
Implementation is the differentiator; Managed Technology Services is the
recurring-revenue path; and KontraktorAI represents the industry specialization.
Suggested package prices are only market-test hypotheses, not approved public
prices. Wait for founder weekly capacity before treating suggested sales-volume
targets as realistic.

The founder estimates 40 working hours per week are available for Gustafta.
Any allocation across sales, delivery, product, finance, and research remains a
planning starting point that should be adjusted to actual workload.

For Part 07, the user finalized:

- Digital products are the entry point toward SaaS; the first proposed product
  is the KontraktorAI Productivity Toolkit.
- Use hybrid SaaS integration plus development of Gustafta-owned core capabilities.
- Target UMKM and industry professionals, entering through construction first.
- Monetize through one-time purchases, subscriptions, and implementation.
- Plan for 2–3 digital products and one SaaS MVP in year one, conditional on
  demand, bootstrap cashflow, founder capacity, and product-readiness checks.
- The proposed product, SaaS, and sales targets are strategic planning aims, not
  proof of availability or guaranteed outcomes.

For Part 08, the user finalized:

- Gustafta will operate a managed marketplace, starting manually; the first-year
  target is initial projects, tenders, matching, and transactions, not a full
  self-service platform.
- Build expert, company, project/tender, partnership, and transaction networks
  gradually and together. Basic membership is free; premium is paid.
- Monetize through consulting, facilitation, commissions, and memberships, with
  early cashflow from consulting and facilitation.
- Provide tender/project information, matching, consultation, and facilitation;
  use referral, project-based, and strategic partnerships.
- Verify credentials based on evidence, protect private documents, cite dated
  official tender sources, avoid guarantees of tender wins, and use compliant
  payment providers rather than holding third-party funds without authority.
- Prices and network/KPI quantities in the blueprint remain hypotheses and
  planning targets, not public prices or achieved results.
- The user chose to record the Gustafta Expert Connect C-B-B-C proposal as a
  recommendation, not a final product decision: consultation + expert directory,
  AI-assisted matching with admin validation, identity/competency/credential
  checks, and staged hybrid monetization.
- Keep the initial expert scope to the five approved KontraktorAI sectors. The
  eight-sector Gustafta taxonomy remains provisional. Do not present sample
  experts, credentials, availability, ratings, or prices as real.
- Consultation intake and a simple founder/admin queue may be P0; AI matching
  never assigns without human review. Payment gateway remains P2; paid services
  and commissions must follow the existing staged, evidence-based marketplace
  model.

For Part 13, the user finalized the KontraktorAI News and CMS strategy:

- Use hybrid news curation across RSS/API, AI assistance, and manual input.
- Use a ready-to-use CMS with custom modules added gradually; WordPress.com
  Business is the selected target for the MVP, not yet purchased or connected.
  A fully custom CMS is not the initial goal.
- Human review is required before publication. Routine stories may be scheduled
  after review; sensitive content needs explicit approval. AI has no independent
  publishing authority.
- Cover news, projects, technology, regulation, analysis, and industry knowledge.
- Distribute in stages across the website/SEO, social and WhatsApp, then
  newsletter and CRM.
- Prioritize qualified leads for Gustafta services as initial monetization;
  advertising is not the primary first-year revenue source.
- These are strategic decisions, not evidence that the CMS, feeds, AI workflow,
  analytics, or distribution integrations are already implemented.
- The user confirmed `kontraktorai.wordpress.com` as the intended WordPress site,
  but said it is not active yet. Its public REST API currently returns
  `invalid_site` 404; treat the feed as unverified until the site becomes public.
