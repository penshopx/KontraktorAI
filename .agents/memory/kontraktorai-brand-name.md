- KontraktorAI is Gustafta's endorsed industry brand, not an independent brand.
- Trilogi Gustafta is the manifesto and methodology; its manifesto is the primary

  sectors and initially serves contractor UMKM and industrial professionals.

  Enter construction first, then energy and electricity, then mining and
  environment; broader expansion follows validation of the initial business,
  products, and operations.

endorsed-brand architecture in the Part 03 strategy.

materials. Keep generic references to the construction industry unchanged.
