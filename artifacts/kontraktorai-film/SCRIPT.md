# KontraktorAI — Brand film

## Director's treatment
- **Promise:** KontraktorAI is Gustafta's endorsed industry brand, opening a practical path for contractor UMKM and industrial professionals to explore an AI-enabled ecosystem.
- **Identity:** Official logo colors #8bdcff, #edf3f5, #9aabb4; deep petroleum-blue fields and restrained mineral/cyan accents are an inferred film palette, not additional official brand colors. Arial from the supplied logo; a technical mono face supports diagrams.
- **Hero:** A luminous cyan construction line that becomes an industry map, then a network aperture and finally the endorsed logo lockup.
- **Motion:** Blueprint line-tracing, hard graphic match cuts, and a circular camera push through the AI Concierge gateway.
- **Arc:** A single line sparks into focus, expands from construction to phased sectors, clarifies the parent/industry relationship, then converges through the Concierge gateway into a resolved brand lockup.
- **Payoff:** The finale inverts the deep technical field into a clean pale field; the genuine supplied logo appears as the final authority, not a generic product mockup.
- **Format/runtime:** 16:9 landscape, 30 seconds planned, Indonesian.

## Pre-code variety review
Five shots move from tight asymmetric blueprint to wide staged sector map, centered brand hierarchy, circular gateway, and pale editorial lockup. Each changes at least two of medium, framing, background system, scale, and dominant mechanic. Each contains multiple events beyond the reveal, with no final 15–20% held motionless. Only Shot 01 uses the blueprint grid; it retires before the next shot. The strongest claim is carried by type and the genuine logo, not audio.
